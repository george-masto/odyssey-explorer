On the west coast, Palaiokastritsa's interlocking coves are the favorite candidate for the beach where Nausicaa found him; off Kanoni, near the airport runway, the tiny cypress islet of Pontikonisi (Mouse Island) passes in local lore for the petrified ship, with the white monastery islet of Vlacherna moored in front of it. The lookout above Kanoni gives you myth, monastery and landing jets in one frame."
