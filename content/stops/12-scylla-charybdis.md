    source: "Od. 12.108–110, 12.223–225"
  - text: "The proverb 'between Scylla and Charybdis' — our 'rock and a hard place' — was fixed for Renaissance Europe by Erasmus, who collected it as Evitata Charybdi in Scyllam incidi: fleeing Charybdis, I fell upon Scylla."
