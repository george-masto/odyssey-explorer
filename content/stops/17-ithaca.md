now_today: "Modern Ithaki answers to the name: whitewashed Vathy curls around one of Greece's deepest natural harbors, and the pebble bay of Dexa just outside it is the traditional Phorcys cove where the Phaeacians laid him ashore still asleep. Skeptics since antiquity have noted the geography fits imperfectly — the boldest modern theory relocates Homer's Ithaca to Paliki on neighboring Kefalonia — but the argument is now part of the island's charm. And every visitor gets sent off with Cavafy's 'Ithaka' (1911), the poem that tells you to wish the road long, for the island's gift is the journey it caused."
