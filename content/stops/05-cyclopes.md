tidbits:
  - text: "The trap is armed at 9.366 and springs at 9.408: the blinded giant roars that Nobody is killing him — Οὖτίς με κτείνει δόλῳ — so his neighbors answer that if no one (μή τις) is hurting him, his pain must be sickness from Zeus, and walk away. Odysseus laughs inwardly at how the name deceived them — and calls the trick his μῆτις, 'cunning': μή τις and μῆτις differ only by an accent and a breath of word-division. English keeps the Noman/no man trick but loses that second pun entirely."
    source: "Od. 9.366, 9.405–414"
  - text: "The wine that fells the giant is loaded chapters earlier: Maron, priest of Apollo at Ismarus, gave Odysseus twelve jars of a vintage so strong it was drunk one cup to twenty measures of water. Polyphemus takes it neat — three bowls, drained in his folly."
    source: "Od. 9.196–211, 9.360–362"
  - text: "The blinded Cyclops prays to his father Poseidon with terrible precision: may Odysseus never reach home — or, if fate insists, may he come 'late and in evil case, after losing all his comrades, in a ship that is another's', to find trouble in his house. Every clause is executed. The curse is the plot of the poem's second half."
    source: "Od. 9.528–535"
  - text: "At Aci Trezza on Sicily's east coast, the jagged basalt Faraglioni dei Ciclopi stand just offshore — by local tradition the very boulders the giant hurled after the taunting ship. They are columnar lava of the Etna system: the volcano that later antiquity chose as the Cyclopes' home smokes directly above them."
    source: "modern Sicilian tradition (Isole dei Ciclopi marine protected area); Etna as Cyclops country: Euripides' satyr-play Cyclops is set there"
